Advanced Level Exercises

We use alt="" for decorative images. Imagine your website has a tiny graphic of a squiggly line just to make the page look pretty.
If you write alt="squiggly line", a blind user using a screen reader is forced to listen to "Image: squiggly line."
It wastes their time and adds no value to the content. Setting alt="" tells the screen reader, "This image is just decoration, skip it entirely.
